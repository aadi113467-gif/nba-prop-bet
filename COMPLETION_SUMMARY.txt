💡 KEY INNOVATION: Stat-Specific Standard Deviations

  Why it matters:
  
  Points (PTS) have HIGH variance:     σ = 5.5
  Assists (AST) are CONSISTENT:       σ = 2.2
  Combo stats (PRA) VERY volatile:    σ = 8.0
  
  Effect on projections:
  
  Same +1.0 edge over line:
  • PTS: Z-score = 1.0/5.5 = 0.18σ  → 54% probability (weak)
  • AST: Z-score = 1.0/2.2 = 0.45σ  → 67% probability (strong!)
  
  Result: Multi-stat projections are MORE ACCURATE
